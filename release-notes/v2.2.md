# AI Exposure Map v2.2

**Identity-safe framing** — we reframed how occupational risk is presented so the site supports growth and agency rather than triggering occupational identity threat. Copy and structure draw on identity-threat research (e.g. Steele 2010), Duhigg’s Three Conversations Model (match the emotional layer before the practical), and Wise Feedback (high standards paired with belief in capability).

## Highlights

- **Insight cards:** “Transition risk” → **Transition window**; “Active displacement” → **Active transformation**
- **Task badges:** “Vulnerable to AI” → **Tasks likely to change**; “Protected from AI” → **Tasks likely to endure**
- **Scoring rubric:** “Exposure” language shifted toward **change expected** / **evolution** framing
- **Workers sections (Analysis & Implications):** added an emotional-first **What This Means for You** callout
- **Wise Feedback–style pairing:** risk scores sit alongside **transferable skills** and **growth pathways** where relevant
- **Questions page:** “displacement” reframed as **transformation** / **dislocation**; stronger **agency** framing for workers
- **Site-wide:** ~40 wording updates across six HTML pages; **version 2.2** in the UI
