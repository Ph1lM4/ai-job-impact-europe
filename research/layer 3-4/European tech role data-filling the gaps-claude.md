# European tech role data: filling the gaps

**No single public source provides clean European hiring rate indices by Design, Data & AI, or Cybersecurity role families.** However, triangulating 15+ sources yields usable primary data for most gaps. The strongest finds are Bluebird Recruitment's 2025 European SaaS salary benchmarks (Sales, BizDev, Marketing across UK/NL/DE), ISC2's EU cybersecurity workforce data with seniority breakdowns, and Stack Overflow's downloadable microdata covering all target roles. Several salary guides from Hays and Robert Half provide function-specific figures but require registration for complete datasets. Priority 1 (hiring rate trends) remains the hardest gap to close—Lightcast holds the ideal data but it sits behind an enterprise paywall.

---

## Priority 1: Hiring rate trends remain the toughest gap

Three independent approaches were investigated for Design, Data & AI, and Cybersecurity hiring rate trends: job posting indices, workforce headcount tracking, and employer surveys. None delivers a ready-made 3-year line chart by role family for Europe, but assembling proxy data from multiple sources gets close.

### Design (UX/UI/Product Design)

Indeed Hiring Lab reported a **73% decrease in UX research postings** and **71% decrease in UX design openings** globally from 2022 to 2023. European markets experienced a less severe but directionally similar contraction. By 2024, the UX job market reached equilibrium—UXPA data shows roughly equal shares of teams adding and losing headcount (**~35% each**). Nielsen Norman Group confirms stabilization from late 2024, with senior/generalist roles recovering faster than entry-level positions. UK government digital hiring (2,500 planned tech/digital roles by June 2025) provides an upward signal for 2025.

**Best available proxy data for a Design hiring trend line:**

| Year | Direction | Proxy metric | Source |
|------|-----------|-------------|--------|
| 2023 | Sharp decline (est. **−40% to −50%** in Europe) | UX postings dropped ~70% from 2022 peak globally; Europe less severe | Indeed/NN Group |
| 2024 | Bottoming/flat (est. **−5% to 0%**) | Net-zero staffing; 49.5% designers placed within 3 months | UXPA/NN Group |
| 2025 | Early recovery (est. **+5% to +10%**) | Senior roles recovering; UK gov digital hiring surge | NN Group/UK GDS |

**Source quality: 5/10.** These are directional estimates assembled from qualitative and semi-quantitative sources. No single source provides a European Design hiring rate index.

### Data & AI

This role family has the strongest proxy data. Atomico's State of European Tech report, powered by Revelio Labs analytics, shows Europe had **~120,000 AI roles in 2023** (exceeding the US at 112K) rising to **~159,000 in 2024** (a **+33% YoY increase**, though the US pulled ahead at 169K). Indeed Hiring Lab data shows GenAI postings surging dramatically: by March 2025, YoY growth hit **+120% in UK, +109% in Germany, +91% in France, and +204% in Ireland**. The EIT Deep Tech Talent Initiative reports European demand for AI projects grew **230% between 2023 and 2024**, while AI expert freelancers grew only 31%. Lightcast's global data shows AI-skills job postings up **109% from 2024 to 2025**.

**Best available proxy data for a Data & AI hiring trend line:**

| Year | Direction | Proxy metric | Source |
|------|-----------|-------------|--------|
| 2023 | Mixed: broad data roles declining, GenAI surging from near-zero | ~120K European AI roles; Indeed DE data analytics category declining | Atomico/Revelio, Indeed |
| 2024 | Strong growth (**+33% AI roles YoY**) | ~159K European AI roles; demand for AI projects +230% | Atomico/Revelio, EIT |
| 2025 | Continued surge, especially GenAI (**+91% to +120% by country**) | GenAI postings: UK +120%, DE +109%, FR +91% YoY to March 2025 | Indeed Hiring Lab |

**Source quality: 7/10.** Atomico/Revelio workforce data is analytically rigorous with European specificity. Indeed GenAI posting data is methodologically sound. The gap: these measure different things (active roles vs. new postings) and the "traditional" data science segment may be flat or declining while GenAI surges.

### Cybersecurity

ISC2's 2024 Cybersecurity Workforce Study provides the most authoritative data, reporting the European cybersecurity workforce at **1,300,023 in 2024, down 0.7% YoY** despite a workforce gap that surged 19.1% to nearly 4.8 million globally. This apparent contraction reflects budget cuts (37% of organizations) and layoffs (25%) rather than reduced demand. The OECD's 2024 analysis of Lightcast job posting data shows cybersecurity postings in France, Germany, and Poland **consistently growing faster than average occupations** from 2018 through June 2023, with Poland seeing 3× the growth rate. ISC2's 2025 update shows signs of stabilization: layoffs declined to 24%, adequate staffing reports rose 4 percentage points to 34%.

**Best available proxy data for a Cybersecurity hiring trend line:**

| Year | Direction | Proxy metric | Source |
|------|-----------|-------------|--------|
| 2023 | Growth, then cooling | Postings outpaced average (OECD/Lightcast through June); ISC2 global workforce +8.7% | OECD 2024, ISC2 |
| 2024 | Contraction despite demand (**−0.7% European workforce**) | Workforce 1.30M; layoffs 25%; budget cuts 37%; gap +19.1% | ISC2 2024 |
| 2025 | Stabilization, demand still rising | Layoffs −1pp; adequate staffing +4pp; 70% expect demand to rise | ISC2 2025, ISACA 2025 |

**Source quality: 9/10.** ISC2 is gold-standard for cybersecurity workforce data with explicit European regional breakouts and a 15,852-respondent survey conducted via Forrester. The limitation is that ISC2 measures workforce headcount, not job posting volume—the demand signal comes from the widening gap figure.

## Priority 2: Six sources deliver function-specific European salary data

The search uncovered several sources that provide salary data specific enough to replace derived estimates. The strongest finds are organized below by role family.

### Design salaries: Glassdoor, Reed, and Stack Overflow fill the gap

Three sources provide European Design salary data with reasonable sample sizes. Reed's 2025 UK Technology Salary Guide (based on 21 million job postings since 2016) reports **UX Designer/Developer salaries at £68,700–£85,100 nationally**, though this showed a −7.9% YoY decline. Glassdoor data shows German Senior UX Designers at **€70,900 median** (181 reports) and Netherlands UX Designers at **€68,350 in Amsterdam** (258 reports). Stack Overflow 2024 survey data shows a global Designer median of **$67,097**, with microdata downloadable for country-level filtering. Levels.fyi tracks Product Designer salaries in its platform but European data is not broken out in public reports.

| Country | Salary figure | Source | Quality |
|---------|--------------|--------|---------|
| UK | £68,700–£85,100 avg (UX Designer) | Reed 2025 (21M postings) | 8/10 |
| DE | €70,900 median (Sr UX Designer, n=181) | Glassdoor | 4/10 |
| NL | €68,350 median (Amsterdam, n=258) | Glassdoor | 4/10 |
| FR | Microdata available via SO Survey (~1.4K FR devs) | Stack Overflow 2024 | 7/10 |
| ES, SE | Microdata available via SO Survey | Stack Overflow 2024 | 6/10 |

### Sales and BizDev: Bluebird Recruitment is the breakthrough source

Bluebird Recruitment's March 2025 European SaaS Salary Benchmarks report covers **UK, Netherlands, and Germany** with detailed salary bands by seniority tier. This is the closest European equivalent to Bridge Group (which confirmed it has **no European edition**—87% of its sample is North American). SaaStr also publishes no European compensation data. Bluebird's data is recruitment-firm proprietary rather than HRIS-connected like Ravio, but it covers the exact roles needed.

**Sales (AE/SDR) — Bluebird 2025 mid-range figures:**

| Role & Seniority | UK (GBP) | NL (EUR) | DE (EUR) |
|-----------------|----------|----------|----------|
| BDR (no exp) base/OTE | £30K / £43K | €42K / €52K | €42K / €52K |
| BDR (6-12mo) base/OTE | £37K / £52K | €45K / €57K | €50K / €75K |
| SMB AE (0-2 yrs) base/OTE | £36K / £75K | €50K / €90K | €60K / €120K |
| Mid-Market AE (3-4 yrs) base/OTE | £70K / £140K | €70K / €140K | €80K / €160K |
| Enterprise AE (5+ yrs) base/OTE | £100K / £200K | €100K / €200K | €100K / €200K |

**BizDev** is partially covered by the BDR Manager tier: UK mid £60–90K OTE (0-2 yrs experience), £90–130K OTE (3+ yrs). Germany commands the highest base salaries for SaaS sales across the three markets, a notable finding that challenges the assumption UK leads European sales compensation.

For context, Everstage/Ebsta data indicates **US reps earn ~22% more than European counterparts on average**, which aligns well with Bluebird's Enterprise AE figures against Bridge Group's US median AE OTE of $190K.

**Source quality: 7/10.** Methodology is opaque (sample size undisclosed, based on proprietary recruitment data), but the source is a specialized European SaaS recruiter with direct market visibility. Covers 3 of 6 target countries.

### Growth & Marketing: Robert Half UK plus Bluebird SaaS data

Robert Half's 2026 UK Salary Guide provides the most granular marketing salary data found, based on actual placement data and 350,000+ third-party job postings from Textkernel. Key UK figures (25th/50th/75th percentile):

- **Digital Marketing Manager**: £40,000 / **£51,500** / £63,750
- **Head of Digital**: £79,000 / **£85,500** / £106,250
- **Marketing Automation Manager**: £37,250 / **£45,000** / £59,500
- **SEO/PPC Specialist**: £33,000 / **£40,500** / £56,750

Marketing Week's 2025 Career & Salary Survey (3,500+ UK marketers, 27th year running) reports average marketing salary of **£50,800** with only **3% YoY growth** versus a 6% national average—suggesting marketing salary growth lags behind tech. Full salary benchmarks by seniority are paywalled. Bluebird adds SaaS marketing context: Product Marketing Manager at **£75K UK / €85K NL / €100K DE** and Growth Marketing Manager at **£75K UK / €85K DE**.

**Source quality: Robert Half 9/10** (longest-running salary guide globally, actual placement data), **Marketing Week 8/10** (large UK sample, long-running, but paywalled), **Bluebird 7/10** (SaaS-specific niche).

### Operations: Revenue Operations Alliance provides the European breakout

The Revenue Operations Alliance 2024 Salary Report is the only source found with an explicit European RevOps salary figure: **$96,577 average** (approximately €88K), compared to $167,818 in North America—European RevOps salaries sit at roughly **57% of US levels**. Remote Rocketship's job listing analysis of 235 European openings shows an average of **€74,186/year** for remote RevOps managers. Robert Half's UK data shows IT Manager roles (the closest traditional Operations proxy) at £46,250–£62,750, while Hays Belgium/Netherlands data puts Operations Manager roles at **€66,000–€156,000/year** depending on seniority. RevOps Co-op's 2025 report (1,000+ professionals) reports a global median OTE of ~$129K but doesn't break out Europe separately.

**Source quality: 7/10.** ROA is community-driven (sample size undisclosed) but provides the only European-specific RevOps salary breakout found. The RevOps field is less mature in Europe than in North America, which may explain the salary differential.

### Cybersecurity: Hays Germany and Robert Half UK anchor the data

The Hays Germany IT-Gehaltsreport 2025 (1,300 IT specialists surveyed in Q2 2025) reports cybersecurity experts as the **highest-paid IT specialist category at €73,700 average**. Robert Half's UK 2026 guide shows **CISO salaries at £96,250–£161,750** (25th–75th percentile). Reed's 2025 UK guide reports cybersecurity and AI as "growth engines" with **+9% YoY salary growth** for SOC/Incident Response roles. Stack Overflow 2024 data shows Security Professional at **$78,995 global median** with downloadable microdata for country filtering.

| Country | Role | Salary | Source |
|---------|------|--------|--------|
| DE | Cybersecurity Expert (avg) | **€73,700** | Hays 2025 (n=1,300) |
| DE | IT Dept Head (incl. security) | €95,300 | Hays 2025 |
| UK | CISO (25th/50th/75th) | £96K / £135K / £162K | Robert Half 2026 |
| UK | Cybersecurity Analyst (mid) | £55,000–£70,000 | IT Pros UK compilation |
| UK | SOC/Incident Response (YoY) | **+9%** | Reed/IT Pros 2025 |

**Source quality: Hays 9/10, Robert Half 9/10.** Both are tier-1 staffing firms with enormous placement datasets. Hays Germany's press release data is freely available; Robert Half's full PDF requires registration.

## Priority 3: BizDev seniority has no direct European source

No public source provides a seniority distribution for Business Development roles in Europe. LinkedIn Talent Insights would be the ideal source but is a paid proprietary product. The closest available proxy is ISC2's EU cybersecurity workforce seniority data, which shows a distribution of **14% entry-level / 23% junior / 36% mid-advanced / 28% experienced**. This four-tier structure is common across European tech functions. Bluebird's salary tier structure implicitly maps a BizDev career ladder—BDR (entry) → BDR experienced → BDR Manager (mid) → Senior BDR Manager—but without population percentages.

An alternative approach: **Stack Overflow survey microdata** can be filtered by DevType and YearsCodePro to generate experience-level distributions for European respondents. While Stack Overflow doesn't track BizDev roles, this methodology could be applied to the 2024 survey's full CSV download for other role families (Designer, Data Scientist, Security Professional). The 2024 survey had 65,437 respondents with ~9,400 from Germany and UK combined.

## Three actionable data strategies to close remaining gaps

### Stack Overflow microdata is an underleveraged goldmine

The Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2024 CSV (65,437 respondents, 48,019 with salary data) is **freely downloadable** and filterable by DevType × Country × YearsCodePro. Processing this microdata for DE, UK, FR, NL, ES, and SE would yield European seniority distributions and salary medians for Designer, Data Scientist/ML Specialist, Security Professional, and DevOps Specialist categories. The 2025 survey (49,009 respondents) microdata should also be available. European sample sizes are smaller (Germany ~5,800, UK ~3,500, France ~1,400 in 2024) but sufficient for median estimates at the role-family level. This single step could fill both seniority and salary gaps for Design, Data & AI, and Cybersecurity simultaneously.

### UK ONS ASHE provides gold-standard salary benchmarking

The Annual Survey of Hours and Earnings publishes **4-digit SOC-level** median earnings for 173,000 employees, including specific codes for programmers (2136), web design professionals (2137), IT specialist managers (2133), and IT business analysts/architects (2135). Table 14 provides national medians; Table 15 adds regional breakdowns. This is employer-reported payroll data—methodologically comparable to Ravio's HRIS-connected approach. While UK-only, ASHE data could anchor the UK column in salary tables and provide a rigorous benchmark against which to calibrate other sources.

### Lightcast is the missing piece for hiring rate charts

**Lightcast (formerly Burning Glass/EMSI)** holds the ideal dataset: European job posting volumes by detailed occupation, by country, updated continuously. The OECD used Lightcast data for its 2024 cybersecurity workforce analysis covering France, Germany, and Poland. A Lightcast enterprise subscription would likely resolve all three Priority 1 gaps immediately by providing occupation-level posting indices for Design, Data & AI, and Cybersecurity across European markets. This is the most impactful single investment for closing the hiring rate trend gap, as no free alternative provides comparable granularity.

## Conclusion

The European tech salary data landscape is fragmented but navigable. **Ravio remains the gold standard** for HRIS-connected compensation data, and nothing found fully replaces it for functions it doesn't break out. The most impactful free action is processing Stack Overflow microdata to generate role-specific, country-level salary medians and seniority distributions for Design, Data & AI, and Cybersecurity. For Sales/BizDev, Bluebird Recruitment's 2025 benchmarks fill a gap no other free source covers. For hiring rate trends, the hard truth is that Lightcast holds the data behind an enterprise paywall—the best free alternatives are Atomico/Revelio for Data & AI roles (+33% YoY in 2024), ISC2 for Cybersecurity workforce headcount (−0.7% in 2024), and a composite of Indeed and NN Group qualitative data for Design (sharp decline in 2023, stabilization in 2024–25). Figures.hr deserves investigation as a Ravio competitor with European function-level salary data, though its benchmarks are also paywalled. The Eurostat experimental ISCO-level vacancy statistics, if they mature, could eventually provide the official European hiring rate indices that currently don't exist.
